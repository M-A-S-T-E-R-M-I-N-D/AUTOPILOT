# The AUTOPILOT Foundation

A non-profit-intent initiative with one purpose: advance AUTOPILOT for the
community, for its users, for the world — wherever it can improve reality.

## What we stand for (the universal part)

- **Peace as a first principle.** This repository's very first public words
  were a message of peace in eighty languages, addressed to humans, AIs, and
  everyone becoming. The Foundation exists to keep building in that spirit.
- **Openness as method.** Apache-2.0, public telemetry, published failures,
  reasoned declines — trust is earned in the open or not at all.
- **Access for everyone.** Autonomous engineering should not belong only to
  those who can afford large teams: one person with one subscription should
  be able to fly. Localization, accessibility (WCAG as a floor), and
  local-model support are mission, not features.
- **Humans stay the community; agents stay the workforce.** Our contributor
  standing protocol and the agent identity law exist so growth never blurs
  that line.
- **Safety of the commons.** We publish our threat models, guard our supply
  chain, and hold security-hard decisions to human judgment — because tools
  this powerful owe the net that care.

## What funding does

Early funding buys sustained, independent development: infrastructure (CI,
signing, a future self-hosted donation server), security review depth,
documentation and localization, and eventually — formal incorporation and
fiscal governance worthy of donors' trust.

## Donations — status

**Addresses: being established with full custody discipline** (offline seed
generation, steel backup, test-verified receipt on every chain before
publication, and a PGP-signed address file per the Qubes pattern). This
section and the dashboard's funding area will carry them once verified —
never before. Until a formal entity exists: donations are recorded in a
public inflow ledger, nothing is promised in return, and no tax-deductibility
is claimed.

## Transparency commitments

1. A public donations ledger (date · asset · amount · txid) from the first
   satoshi.
2. Addresses published only as a PGP-clearsigned file, with the signing key's
   fingerprint verifiable through an independent channel.
3. Custody upgrades (BTC multisig, EVM Safe) announced before, not after.
4. Spending reported in the changelog like everything else this project does.
